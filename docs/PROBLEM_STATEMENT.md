# Problem Statement

## Existing Problem

Brands and marketing teams need to identify YouTube creators who are relevant to their products and campaigns.

Selecting creators only by subscriber count may overlook engagement, content relevance, audience response, and creator activity.

Collecting and comparing these indicators manually can take considerable time.

## Challenges

- Discovering creators in a specific niche.
- Comparing channel reach and performance.
- Understanding engagement beyond subscriber count.
- Evaluating textual relevance to a brand or product.
- Reviewing public audience comments.
- Organizing creator information for comparison.

## Proposed Solution

Creator Intelligence Pro combines public YouTube data collection, engagement analytics, comment sentiment analysis, brand relevance scoring, and creator ranking in a single Streamlit dashboard.

## Expected Outcome

A decision-support platform that helps users discover and compare YouTube creators using multiple measurable indicators.

## Scope

The current implementation focuses on publicly available YouTube information. Private audience demographics and verified campaign ROI are outside the current scope.
